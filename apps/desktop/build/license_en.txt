4. YOU ALONE CONTROL YOUR MONEY

LBC Wallet is non-custodial: your keys are created and stored only on your own

* Nobody - not the developers, not LBRY, not anyone else - can recover a lost
  recovery phrase or password, reverse or cancel a payment, or freeze or
  return funds.
* If you lose your recovery phrase and your device or password, your funds are
  lost forever. Write the recovery phrase on paper, keep it private and safe,
  and never type it into websites.
* Anyone who gets your recovery phrase, or malware on your device, can take
  your funds. Keeping your device secure is your responsibility.
* Payments are final. Always check the address before sending.

5. MINING

* Mining LBC with a normal computer (CPU) usually earns very little and often
  costs more in electricity than it earns. Estimates shown in the app are
  rough and not a promise of income.
* Mining runs your processor at high load for long periods. It produces heat
  and noise, uses more power, and can shorten the life of your hardware or
  affect its warranty.
* Do not mine on computers or networks you do not own or have no permission to
  use (for example your employer's, school's or a cloud provider's). It may
  break their rules or the law.
* Mining or holding cryptocurrency may be restricted or illegal where you
  live. Check first.
* Mining pools are run by third parties who may change fees, go offline or
  fail to pay.

6. BUILT-IN FULL NODE

The optional built-in node runs lbcd, LBRY's official full-node software,
unmodified. It downloads and stores the whole LBRY blockchain (well over 100
GB), uses a lot of bandwidth, disk and processor time, and - when you allow
incoming connections - asks your router to open a port (UPnP) so other nodes
can connect to it. Check your disk space, data plan and network rules before
turning it on.

7. SWAPS AND THE MARKET (ORDER BOARD)

* Atomic swaps (the Swap page) and trades on the Market lock your coins in
  contracts on the LBC and Bitcoin blockchains. If a swap does not finish,
  your coins come back only after the lock time (up to 48 hours), and only
  while the app runs with that wallet unlocked. Bugs in this code can lose the
  coins involved. Swaps and the Market are new and not audited.
* The Market is not an exchange, a broker or a marketplace operator. Nobody
  runs it, holds coins, checks the people who post or take offers, sets or
  guarantees prices, settles disputes or reverses trades. Offers come from
  anonymous strangers and can be misleading, far from the market price, or
  never honoured. A trade can keep your coins locked for up to 48 hours and
  cost network fees even if the other side never completes it. The market
  price shown for comparison comes from third parties and may be wrong or out
  of date.
* Offers and trade messages travel through Nostr relays run by third parties,
  who can see your IP address and when your keys exchange messages (not their
  content), and may delay, drop or censor them.
* Trading crypto may be regulated where you live (licences, identity checks,
  taxes, reporting, or bans on some coins or services). You alone are
  responsible for whether you may trade, with whom, and for declaring it. The
  project does not provide, and is not, a regulated financial service.
* Before using the Market the app shows its own warning, which you must
  accept.

8. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND PRIVACY

To work, the app connects to services run by other people: public LBRY wallet
servers ("hubs"), mining pools, Bitcoin explorers for swaps, Nostr relays for
the Market, and - only if you turn on fiat prices or open the Market - price
services (CoinPaprika, MEXC). Links you open go to GitHub or a block explorer.
Their operators can see your IP address and which addresses or transactions
you ask about, and may log it. The app itself sends no analytics or telemetry.
For more privacy, use your own node or route connections through Tor (SOCKS5).
